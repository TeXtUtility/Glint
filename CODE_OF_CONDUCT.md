# Community terms

Glint's issues, pull requests and discussions are for making Glint better.

- **Be decent.** Criticise code and ideas, not people. No harassment, slurs or personal attacks.
- **Keep it private.** Don't post anyone's personal details, or transcripts and screenshots of other people's calls.
- **Don't sell here.** No ads, and no promoting paid products built on Glint's code, which its [license](LICENSE) doesn't allow.
- **Use Glint fairly.** Get consent before recording people where the law asks for it, and don't use Glint where outside help isn't allowed.

Maintainers may edit or remove anything that breaks these terms, and block people who keep breaking them. To report something, mention @TeXtUtility in an issue, or use GitHub's Report content option for anything you'd rather not post in public.
